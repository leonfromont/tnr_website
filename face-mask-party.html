Good to Know

A few
details

Who is this evening for?
This is a private, invitation-only evening for our valued clients. Your invitation is just for you, though if you’d love to bring a friend, drop us a line and we’ll see what spaces remain.
Do I need to prepare anything?
Not at all. Arrive with a freshly cleansed, makeup-free face if you can, so your skin is ready for the masks. We’ll take care of everything else.
I have sensitive skin or allergies, is that okay?
Absolutely. Our therapists will tailor the masks to your skin on the night. If you have known sensitivities or allergies, let us know in advance at admin@truenorthritual.com so we can plan ahead.
What is your refund or transfer policy?
If your plans change, please get in touch as early as you can and we’ll do our best to help.
Where exactly do I go on the night?
We’re on Level 2 at 418 Princes Street, Central Dunedin — head up and we’ll be ready for you.

Still wondering something?

We’d love to hear from you. Email admin@truenorthritual.com or call 020 4080 2259.
